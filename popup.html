Help & Support
Getting started

Open your own Instagram profile, then select Scan Instagram. Keep the tab open until scanning finishes.

Refresh just one list

Tools > Refresh Followers or Refresh Following. The other saved list stays unchanged. Results may reflect different scan dates.

Stopping a scan

Click Stop Scan. Any unfinished scan is discarded and previously saved lists are kept.

Incorrect results or stuck scans

Refresh your Instagram profile and retry. A partial scan will not replace a saved list. If it still isn't working, go into Tools and try refreshing each list separately.

Privacy and backups

Saved usernames and lists are stored in Chrome extension local storage. Use Tools to export, restore, or delete them. The extension does not require your Instagram password.
